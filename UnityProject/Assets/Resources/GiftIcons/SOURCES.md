# Guide icons

Numeric PNG filenames are original-size TikTok gift icons from the public webcast catalog fetched 2026-11-01. Metadata and original image URLs are in TikTokBridge/config/gift-catalog.json. Files were decoded from WebP to PNG without resizing for Unity compatibility. They identify gifts in the local guide; no stock or resale license is claimed.

like-heart.png is Kenney Particle Pack symbol_01 (CC0), rendered as a generic pink heart for free likes. See Assets/Resources/Vfx/KENNEY-LICENSE.txt. This is deliberately separate from paid TikTok heart gifts.
